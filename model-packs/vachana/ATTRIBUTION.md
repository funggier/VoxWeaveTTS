# VachanaTTS — provenance, usage and limitations

**Model publisher:** VIZINTZOR  
**Original model source:** https://huggingface.co/VIZINTZOR/VachanaTTS  
**Pinned revision:** `6fa5f03a26f8ec0c0e0582e9e6812efa84d38688`  
**Source license declaration:** `mit` in the Hugging Face model metadata and README.  
**Speech engine:** VITS / Piper-style model, 4 ONNX voices (th_f_1, th_f_2, th_m_1, th_m_2).  
**VoxWeaveTTS package:** `vachana-thai-four-voices` v1.0.0.

## Rights stated by the source

- The model publisher labels the model repository **MIT**. MIT grants redistribution with attribution and the copyright/license notice, without requiring commercial use. This is an upstream **declaration**, not an independent rights audit.
- The model card says its training implementation uses PiperTTS. The license of the software used for training and the license of its separately released weights are not automatically identical.
- The model card does **not** identify all training datasets, individual voice contributors, or a separately signed license/copyright assignment for each voice. **These rights remain unverified.**
- A separate VIZINTZOR model based on MMS-TTS does not establish that these four Vachana voices derive from MMS; do not infer either relationship without evidence.

## Redistribution conditions and user responsibility

Retain the original source reference, publisher credit, MIT license declaration and this file. Do not claim that VoxWeaveTTS trained or owns these voices. If redistributing or using voices in a particular jurisdiction, verify that your use respects the rights of the relevant contributors and training data. **We provide the files as-is without a warranty or commercial-use certification.** This disclaimer does not override any upstream license obligation.

The `.vwmodel` is a data-only repack of weights previously published by the model publisher; it contains no executable code. If the publisher changes/revokes a rights declaration or a rights holder gives notice, the pack should be reviewed and may be unpublished.
